Describe & Classify Rocks

Geologists start by observing. Hover over the rock to use your cursor as a hand lens and zoom in on the grains. Then describe the rock’s grain size, grain shape, color, and texture, and use the clues to decide whether it is Igneous, Sedimentary, or Metamorphic.

Igneous

Forms from cooled magma or lava. Hard, usually with no layers. (Granite, Obsidian)

Sedimentary

Forms from compacted sediment. Often crumbly and layered. (Sandstone, Limestone, Coal)

Metamorphic

Forms when other rocks are changed by heat and pressure. Hard; may or may not have layers. (Slate, Marble)
